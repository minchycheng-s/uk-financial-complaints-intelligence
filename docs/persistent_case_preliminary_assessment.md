# Preliminary analytical assessment of persistent cases

## What this assessment can and cannot do

This assessment helps a non-specialist understand why the six cases remain in
the investigation queue. It uses published aggregate FCA data and transparent
rule evidence.

It can identify persistent patterns and the questions a business owner should
answer. It cannot identify individual customer harm, prove misconduct, explain
internal operating processes or authorise remediation. For that reason, every
root cause remains `not_determined` and every recommendation is an escalation
for informed business review.

## PCR-001 — SCOTTISH WIDOWS LIMITED, Investments

- Priority classification appears in all ten reporting periods.
- High provision context rate and high upheld percentage appear in all ten
  periods and persist in nine.
- Opened complaints fell from their historical peak, and the latest provision
  context rate is also below its peak.
- The latest upheld percentage remains high at 88.34%.

**Interpretation:** this is a persistent peer-relative outcome pattern, not
simply rising complaint volume. Public data does not show why complaints are
upheld or whether business mix explains the comparison.

**Review question:** which complaint categories drive upheld outcomes, how is
the context denominator constructed, and what remediation has already been
attempted?

## PCR-002 — British Gas Services Limited, Insurance and Pure Protection

- Priority classification appears in nine consecutive periods from 2021-H2.
- High provision context rate and high upheld percentage appear in all ten
  periods and persist in nine.
- Complaint volume and the provision context rate have declined materially.
- The latest upheld percentage remains 93.07%.

**Interpretation:** falling volume does not remove the persistent complaint-
outcome signal. The result requires examination of upheld reasons and product
mix rather than an assumption that the declining counts resolve the issue.

**Review question:** are the upheld outcomes concentrated in a product,
complaint reason, reporting population or historical process that has not yet
worked through the data?

## PCR-003 — ManyPets Ltd, Insurance and Pure Protection

- Priority classification appears in six of eight available periods.
- Provision and intermediation context-rate peer signals recur.
- The upheld percentage rose from 31.45% in 2022-H1 to 71.98% in 2025-H2.
- Opened complaints peaked earlier and declined in the latest period.

**Interpretation:** the evidence spans both context rates and complaint
outcomes. Provision and intermediation measures must be reviewed separately
because they have different business meanings and denominators.

**Review question:** does the increased uphold percentage reflect a complaint-
handling change, portfolio mix, reporting practice or another supported cause?

## PCR-004 — OVO HOME SERVICES LTD, Insurance and Pure Protection

- Priority classification appears in five periods, with `review` in the other
  periods.
- High upheld percentage appears in nine periods and persists in seven.
- Latest opened complaints remain within the historical range.
- The latest upheld percentage is 75.59%, and the provision peer signal
  persists.

**Interpretation:** this is a recurring relative outcome and context pattern,
not an exceptional latest-period volume event.

**Review question:** why does the firm remain high relative to its product
peers, and are period-to-period comparisons based on consistent reporting
populations?

## PCR-005 — Elderbridge Limited, Home Finance

- The sequence moves from `no_current_signal`, through `review`, to
  `priority_review` in the latest four periods.
- The provision context rate increased from 25.04 to 49.51.
- The high provision peer signal appears in nine periods.
- A complaint-timeliness signal also persists in the latest sequence.

**Interpretation:** the emerging priority pattern combines business-context
and complaint-aging evidence. Complaint volume itself is not rising
materially.

**Review question:** do the context denominator, servicing population and
complaints remaining open beyond eight weeks explain the escalation?

## PCR-006 — Scottish Equitable Plc, Investments

- Priority classification emerged in the latest three periods.
- The latest score of 11 combines provision context, upheld outcome and
  complaints beyond eight weeks.
- The upheld percentage increased from 49.50% in 2021-H1 to 88.11% in
  2025-H2.
- Opened complaints fell from the immediately preceding period.

**Interpretation:** the recent escalation is supported by several outcome
families, not by simple complaint growth.

**Review question:** what changed around 2024-H2 in complaint aging, uphold
reasons, product mix or context reporting?

## Recommended handling

1. A complaints or conduct-risk business owner should take ownership of each
   case.
2. The owner should obtain internal complaint-category, root-cause and
   remediation evidence that is unavailable in the public data.
3. The owner should answer the case-specific review questions above.
4. An independent reviewer should challenge the conclusion.
5. Only then should `review_status` move to `completed`.

Until those steps occur, these records remain preliminary analytical
assessments and the methodology remains pending business review.
