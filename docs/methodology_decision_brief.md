# Warning methodology decision brief

## Analyst recommendation

Do **not** approve the warning method for unattended operational decision
making yet. It is suitable for a controlled analytical pilot because its
rules, family controls, persistence logic, coverage labels and caveats are
transparent and reproducible.

Approval should require:

1. a named business owner and independent reviewer;
2. agreement that scores prioritise review and do not measure harm;
3. acceptance of product-specific coverage limitations;
4. documented handling of insufficient evidence and confirmed source
   anomalies;
5. outcome monitoring and threshold review on future reporting periods; and
6. a route to pause or amend the method if false positives or unintended
   behaviours emerge.

## Decision status

`pending_business_review`. The repository supplies the evidence and
recommendation, but an analyst cannot grant business approval.
