# Rule-driver assessment

## Latest-period result

The 225 triggered rule rows in 2025-H2 are concentrated in:

| Rule | Triggers | Persistent | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Complaints beyond eight weeks | 63 | 33 | Largest workload and materially persistent |
| High upheld percentage vs peers | 39 | 28 | Strongest persistence share among major rules |
| High provision context rate vs peers | 33 | 24 | Persistent, but denominator meaning needs business review |
| Upheld percentage deterioration | 27 | 2 | Usually a new movement rather than persistence |
| Exceptional opened-complaint growth | 26 | 5 | Important volume screen, not sufficient alone |

Counts overlap because an observation may trigger several rules.

## Review framework

1. Timeliness: investigate complaint types remaining open beyond eight weeks,
   operational backlogs and reporting-window effects.
2. Upheld outcomes: investigate complaint reasons, product mix and whether
   firms agreed wholly or partly with consumers.
3. Context rates: confirm denominator and business meaning. Provision and
   intermediation measures must remain separate.
4. Volume: distinguish sustained growth from a low prior-period base and
   changes in reporting population.

Public data identifies the pattern but cannot determine operational root
cause. The business review therefore remains open.
