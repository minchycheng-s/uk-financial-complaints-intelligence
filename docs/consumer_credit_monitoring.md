# Consumer Credit monitoring assessment

## Why this needs a separate process

In 2025-H2, Consumer Credit has 26 `monitor` and 15 `review` observations.
It has no broad-coverage observations because comparable timeliness and
context-rate evidence is unavailable. Absence from the priority band is
therefore not evidence of low risk.

## Proposed monitoring process

1. Review the 41 monitor/review observations each half-year.
2. Rank sustained and exceptional complaint-volume growth first.
3. Check current volume as well as percentage growth so a low prior base does
   not dominate.
4. Escalate repeated volume signals for business investigation.
5. Keep Consumer Credit results separate from the multi-family priority
   classification until a product-specific methodology is approved.
6. Record the business explanation and outcome; do not infer customer harm
   from volume alone.

The initial queue is
`data/processed/governance/action_evidence/consumer_credit_monitoring_queue.csv`.
It is an analyst work queue, not an automated adverse classification.
